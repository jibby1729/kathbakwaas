About

Made by Jibran Iqbal Shah. Inspired by the KATHE AI 2026 contest.

Our model tops open Kashmiri translation benchmarks, ahead of the ranked KATHE contest models, Microsoft Translator, and the frontier closed-source models tested.

Switch off diacritics to hide diacritics like zabar (◌َ), zer (◌ِ), and pesh (◌ُ).

Models: Koshur-Sarvam-4B and Koshur-JibDiacritizer-152M.

Voices use Bolbosh, made by Gaash Lab at NIT Srinagar.

Translations may contain errors as this is a neural network based translator.
