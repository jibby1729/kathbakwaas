# Website fonts

## Latin Modern Roman

Unmodified OpenType fonts from Latin Modern 2.005 (21 March 2021), distributed
with TeX Live. Based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern, by Bogusław Jackowski
and Janusz M. Nowacki. Copyright 2003–2021 B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki.

Included files: `lmroman10-regular.otf`, `lmroman10-bold.otf`, and
`lmroman10-italic.otf`. These are a selection from the upstream font family.

License: `GUST-FONT-LICENSE.TXT`. Upstream information:
`README-Latin-Modern.TXT` and <https://www.ctan.org/pkg/lm>.

## Noto Naskh Arabic

`noto-naskh-arabic.ttf` supplies the Arabic-script characters missing from Latin
Modern, including the Kashmiri output. License: `OFL.txt`.

All fonts are served locally by the application.
